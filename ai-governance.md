# AI Governance

## Overview
AI governance ensures that artificial intelligence systems operate responsibly, ethically, and securely.
This section defines the policies, controls, and frameworks that guide AI development and deployment.

## Governance Principles
-** Transparency: ** Maintain clear documentation of model design, data sources, and decisions logic.
-** Accountability: **Assign ownership for AI outcomes and ensure auditability of model actions.
-** Fairness: ** Mitigate bias in training data and model behavior through continuous evaluation.
-** Privacy: ** Protect user data and ensure compliance with data protection laws.
-** Security: ** Protect user data and ensure compliance with data protection laws.
-** Compliance: ** Align with regulatory frameworks such as NIST AI RMF and ISO/IEC 42001.

## Governance Controls
-Establish and AI Risk Register to track vulnerabilities and compliance gaps.
-Conduct regular AI audits and bias assessments.
-Define escalation paths for ethical or security incidents.
-Require model validation before deployment.
-Enforce version control and change management for all AI models.















## Tools Used
-** AI Compliance Manager** - track governance metrics and audit results.
-** Bias Check** - evaluates fairness and bias in datasets.
-** Model Audit** - verifies model integrity and compliance.
-** Policy Sim** - simulates governance scenarios and risk outcomes.

## Roles and Responsibilites
-**AI Governance Lead: ** Oversees policy creation and compliance enforcement.
-** Data Protection Officer: ** Ensures privacy and regulatory adherence.
-**AI Developer:** Implements secure and ethical coding practices.
-** Auditor: ** Reviews governance reports and validates controls.
